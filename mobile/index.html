What it does

Eight tools. One phone.

  1. 01

    3D body scan

    Capture a real 3D model of the body in seconds, straight from the phone.

  2. 02

    Import references

    Pull in reference images and mood boards to shape the direction.

  3. 03

    AI tattoo ideas

    Generate concepts with AI when you start from a blank page.

  4. 04

    Place on the scan

    Drop a design onto the 3D model to test size and placement on the real body.

  5. 05

    Basic editing

    Quick adjustments to get the idea close before it reaches the studio.

  6. 06

    Built for clients

    A simple, approachable interface anyone can use. No training, no manual.

  7. 07

    Share with your artist

    Send scans, references and ideas to the artist in a single tap.

  8. 08

    Synced to iPad

    Everything you capture appears automatically inside DremonPro Studio.

Before the appointment

Clients scan themselves before they book. The artist opens a true 3D model of the body, already sized and placed.
