Mission

Bring the workflow back to the bench.

The tools tattoo artists use today were never built for tattooing.

Artists jump between apps made for graphic design, printing, illustration, and photo editing — forcing an entire tattoo workflow through software that was never designed for skin.

Every session includes unnecessary exports, resizing, stencil corrections, reprints, and manual adjustments.

DREMONPRO brings the entire process into one connected workflow.

Scan. Design. Fit. Stencil. Print. Tattoo.

All inside a single system built for the bench.

Built through real tattoo sessions, convention testing, and years of workflow frustration, DREMONPRO was created to rethink how tattoo artists design, place, and prepare tattoos in the modern era.

The goal was never to make another drawing app.

The goal was to build the first true operating system for tattooing.

Tattooing evolved.The software never did.

Tattooing became more advanced.

Bodies became larger canvases.

Clients became more demanding.

But the tools stayed fragmented.

DREMONPRO was built to close that gap.

FAQ

Common questions about the team and timeline

Who founded DREMONPRO?

DREMONPRO was founded in 2025 by a small team of working tattoo artists and software engineers across Europe. Founding artists from Berlin, Tokyo and São Paulo joined as creative partners and have been bench-testing the tool with their own clients since 2024.

Where is the company based?

The legal entity is registered in the EU. The team is remote-first by design, with active studios in Berlin, Lisbon and Tokyo. The contact address for legal correspondence is on our Legal hub.

Are you hiring?

We hire selectively for engineering, ML, and customer success. If you've worked in or alongside a tattoo studio, that's a significant plus. Send a cover note and CV to jobs@dremonpro.com.

Have you raised funding?

DREMONPRO is currently bootstrapped, with a small seed round closing alongside the beta launch. We don't share investor details publicly — press queries can email press@dremonpro.com or use the contact form.

How can I get involved before beta?

Join the waitlist. Founding seats include lifetime locked-in pricing, day-one beta access on June 17 2026, and a direct line to the team via the founders' Slack.
