FAQ

Common questions about the features

Do I need new hardware to use DREMONPRO?

No. DREMONPRO runs on the iPad Pro (M2 or newer) or iPhone 15 Pro or newer that you most likely already own. There are no calibration rigs, dongles, or external sensors to buy. On older devices without LiDAR, photogrammetry kicks in automatically using the camera and the workflow stays the same.

Which features work offline?

3D body scanning, design, placement, stencil generation and printing all run on-device and work fully offline — critical for conventions, tours, and studios with patchy internet. Cloud sync, real-time collaboration and shared client databases require a connection.

How does the AI learn my personal style?

You upload your own stencils, line work, and reference sketches. The model trains a per-artist fine-tune that generates variations in your hand — not generic AI output. Your training data is encrypted and never used to train shared models or any other artist's account.

Can I disable features I don't use?

Yes. Every feature can be toggled off in the studio settings — voice commands, gesture controls, style learning, AR client preview — so your interface stays focused on the tools you actually use day-to-day.

Will all seven features be available at beta launch?

All seven core technologies ship on June 17, 2026. Founding artists receive every feature update at no extra cost for the lifetime of their account.
