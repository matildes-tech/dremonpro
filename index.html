What is DREMONPRO

The tattoo workflow rebuilt from the skin up.

DREMONPRO scans a real 3D model of your client, lets you design directly on their body, and prints a stencil at the exact size you placed it. Seven technologies in one tool, built with tattoo artists, for tattoo artists.

What it does

Seven technologies.

01 — 3D Body Scan

3D Body Scan

Scan the body in seconds using a phone or tablet. Preview tattoos directly on a real 3D model and see exactly how the design wraps, bends, and flows before tattooing.

02 — Touchless Control

Touchless Control

Move, zoom, rotate, and control the canvas without touching the screen. Built for clean workflow during tattoo sessions using gestures and voice commands.

03 — Anatomy Fitting

Anatomy Fitting

Designs automatically adapt to the client's real anatomy and body scale. No more guessing sizes or adjusting stencils by hand.

04 — Instant Stencil

Instant Stencil

Turn any finished design into a ready-to-print stencil instantly. Generated at the exact size and placement shown on the 3D body scan.

05 — Style Learning

Style Learning

Train the AI using your own tattoos and stencil work. Generate designs that actually look like your hand instead of generic AI art.

06 — Live Collabs

Live Collabs

Work on the same tattoo project with another artist in real time from anywhere in the world. Draw, edit, and create together on the same canvas.

07 — Smart Print

Smart Print

Print tattoos at any scale without manual resizing. Large designs are automatically split into perfectly aligned A4 pages, ready to print instantly.

How it works

Scan. Design. Place. Calibrate. Stencil. Print. Pin. Tattoo.

Scan the Body

Create a real 3D scan of your client using only a phone or tablet. The body becomes your working canvas, with real proportions and anatomy.

Design Directly on the Skin

Draw directly on the 3D body model instead of flat photos. See how the tattoo bends, wraps, and flows before the session even starts.

Adjust Placement in Real Scale

Move, resize, and position the tattoo exactly where it belongs. Because the scan is true-to-scale, what you see is what will fit on the body.

Calibrate to True Scale

DREMONPRO locks the design to real-world dimensions using the 3D mesh as ground truth. Sleeve continuity, joint flex zones and fold lines all stay accurate.

Generate the Perfect Stencil

One tap turns the placed design into a ready-to-apply stencil — lines optimised for thermal transfer, tiled to A4 with precise registration marks.

Print Direct from the iPad

Send the stencil straight to a thermal printer over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Multi-page designs print in sequence, pre-aligned for assembly.

Pin the Stencil to the Body

Use the AR overlay to align the printed stencil onto the exact placement you approved on the 3D model. The iPad highlights any drift in real time.

Start the Session Prepared

Placement approved, stencil pinned, sizing accurate. The hard part is solved — you focus purely on tattooing while gesture and voice controls keep the sterile field intact.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When can I get the apps?

The beta is rolling out now. Download DremonPro Mobile, or leave your email, and we'll bring you in as we open access. Free while we're in beta.

How does the 3D body scan work?

LiDAR on iPad Pro (M2+) or iPhone 15+ builds a 3D mesh in under 10 seconds. On-device, encrypted, no calibration.

Will my designs stay private?

Yes. End-to-end encrypted, on-device by default, never used to train shared models. Cloud sync is opt-in.

Is it one app or two?

Two, one account. DremonPro Mobile (iPhone) captures the body and the idea; Studio for iPad is where you design, measure and print. Everything syncs between them.

Do I need new hardware?

No. iPad Pro (M2+) or iPhone 15+ is everything you need.

What does it cost?

Free while we're in beta. After launch, see the pricing page for plans.
