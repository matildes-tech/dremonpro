FAQ

Common questions about pricing

Is there a free trial?

Founding artists on the waitlist get a 30-day full-feature trial from June 17. After 30 days, you choose a plan or your account moves to a limited free tier — up to 5 stencil generations per month. No payment method is required to begin the trial.

What does "founding pricing" mean?

The first 327 founding seats lock in their plan price forever. As we add features and scale, list prices will rise — but your founding rate never changes for the life of your account.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your dashboard at any time. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the billing period; annual plans can be prorated.

Do you offer team and studio pricing?

The Studio plan is per-seat with multi-seat collaboration, shared assets, white-label and team workflows. For larger rollouts, contact sales for custom enterprise terms.
