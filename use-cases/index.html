FAQ

Common questions from working artists

Can I use DREMONPRO for cover-ups over existing tattoos?

Yes. The scan captures the existing tattoo as a layer, and you can preview the cover-up overlaid on top with adjustable density and contrast. Clients see exactly how the new piece reads against the old work before you commit to anything.

Does the client AR preview need an iPhone?

Any modern smartphone with AR support works (iOS 14+ and recent Android). For clients on older devices, you can share a 3D preview link that opens in any browser, or show the preview yourself on your studio iPad during the consult.

How does DREMONPRO work at conventions or for walk-ins?

The whole core workflow runs offline on an iPad Pro. You can scan, design, preview and print a stencil with no internet, no calibration rig, no Wi-Fi at the convention. Studios commonly cut their walk-in turnaround time by 60–70%.

Can a studio share clients across multiple artists?

Yes. Studio and Enterprise plans include a shared client database with per-artist permissions, role-based access, and studio branding on client-facing previews and reports. Each artist still owns their own ML model and design history.
